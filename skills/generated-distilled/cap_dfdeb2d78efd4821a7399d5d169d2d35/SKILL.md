# Evaluate Job-Posting Evidence（招聘岗位情报评估）

## When to use
Use this guidance when a company-research or analysis task asks whether job postings on recruitment platforms such as Boss直聘（Boss Zhipin）or 猎聘（Liepin）are a valuable information source, or when a job posting is being used as evidence about a company.

## What job postings can support
Treat such postings as worth consulting and of relatively high value. They can supplement and help verify:
- 投入方向（investment / strategic direction）
- 组织职责（organizational responsibilities）
- 技术栈（technology stack）
- 扩张节奏（expansion rhythm）

They are especially suitable for verifying 负责人（leadership / owner）positions against the existing system picture.

## What job postings cannot prove
A job posting only proves 招聘需求（hiring demand）. It cannot prove:
- 采购意向（procurement intent）
- 真实痛点（real pain points）

Do not present a posting as direct evidence of procurement intent or actual customer pain points.

## Handling requirements
When using a posting as evidence, preserve:
- 岗位原文（original posting text）
- 发布日期（publish date）
- 公司主体（company entity）

Then cross-verify the posting against the official website（官网）and interviews（访谈）before drawing conclusions.

## Boundaries
- Applies to evaluating job postings as a research signal; do not extend it to claims about procurement intent or pain points that this evidence does not support.
- Derived from a single completed turn; applicability to materially different research tasks is not established.
- This guidance implies no account access, credentials, or platform-side actions.
