# B2B Daily Actionable Results (Service Positioning Principle)

## Purpose
Guide positioning and scoping of a B2B service offer built around a daily "actionable results" deliverable — e.g., business opportunities and tender analysis — rather than selling AI itself as the product.

## When to use
- The user expresses intent to break down daily data silos (打通日常的数据壁垒) and produce a daily service report focused on one direction such as business opportunities (商业机会) or tender/bid analysis (标讯分析).
- The user explicitly contrasts B2B with the consumer (C-side) segment, emphasizing that what is delivered is results, with AI as an incidental part of the experience.

## Core principles (from the episode)
1. **Sell the actionable result, not AI.** For B2B, position the offer as "actionable results delivered to the decision-maker every day" (每天送到老板手里的可行动结果). AI is a byproduct of the experience, unlike the consumer side.
2. **Define a minimal entry point.** Connect internal and external enterprise data to produce a daily output covering: business opportunities (商业机会), tender matching (标讯匹配), customer dynamics (客户动态), and next-step suggestions (下一步建议).
3. **Narrow scope first, expand only after validation.** Start with a single high-value direction. Prove that customers will read it, act on it, and renew before expanding data sources.

## Boundaries
- This skill captures a positioning/scoping principle from a single strategy discussion. It is not evidence that any data pipeline, report delivery, or customer validation exists.
- Do not claim the approach has been proven effective or that any service is live.
- Do not assume enterprise data, tender feeds, or customer data are available or authorized; those require separate authorization and evidence.
- Do not extend to consumer (C-side) positioning, which the user explicitly distinguished in the episode.
