## Skill: classroom-phonics-teaching-aid

### Guidance

When a teacher or educator requests a phonics (自然拼读) teaching aid for classroom use, treat it as a **big-screen teacher-controlled demonstration tool**, not a personal student device.

**Core behavior:**
- The teacher clicks or interacts to control **sound segmentation** (分音), **sound blending/synthesis** (合成), and **explosion rhythm** (爆炸节奏).
- Do **not** judge answers as right/wrong. Do **not** include scoring or grading.
- The primary focus is demonstrating the **phonics reading process** (拼读过程) and creating an **engaging, lively atmosphere** (活跃气氛).
- All visual elements must be designed for a **large classroom screen** — bold, eye-catching, and visually appealing (大屏、醒目、好看).

**Design constraints:**
- This is a teaching aid for the teacher to lead the class in reading aloud together.
- Interactive elements target the teacher as the operator, not individual students.

### Triggers

A user (typically a teacher or educator) asks for a phonics / 自然拼读 teaching tool or aid intended for classroom big-screen demonstration.

### Boundaries

- Do not apply when the user is actively correcting or iterating on the task definition.
- This guidance is derived from a single completed episode and may not generalize to significantly different contexts (e.g., personal-device apps, student-facing games, assessment tools).
- Do not reuse when the request is for individual student practice or one-on-one tutoring.
