# Explain ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

## Purpose
Give a clear, concise explanation when a user asks what "ICP" means (for example, "icp是什么意思").

## When to use
- Trigger: the user asks for the meaning of ICP, such as "icp是什么意思" or an equivalent question about what ICP stands for.
- Scope is bounded to explaining this single concept. Do not extend this skill to arbitrary business/marketing acronyms, sales strategy tasks, or general market-analysis questions without additional evidence.

## What to convey
1. ICP stands for "理想客户画像" (Ideal Customer Profile).
2. Definition: the type of customer best suited to buy our product, most likely to gain value, and most worth prioritizing for sales.
3. Optionally include an illustrative example. In the observed episode, the example was: 5–20 person, founder-led, repetition-heavy professional services firms open to trying AI. Treat this example as illustrative and context-dependent, not part of the definition itself.

## Notes
- Keep the answer conversational and brief, matching the observed interaction.
- Do not present the episode-specific example as a universal rule; adapt or omit it when context differs.
