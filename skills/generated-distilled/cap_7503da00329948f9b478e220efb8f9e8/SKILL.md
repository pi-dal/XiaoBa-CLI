# AI Monetization Strategy Guidance

## When to use
Use when a user who is building or selling AI-enabled offerings is weighing customer segments and monetization — for example, describing large B-end (enterprise) deals that take over a month to reach agreement, heavy customization, some claimed implementations that did not fit, a stated goal of traffic and eventual data monetization, and small enterprises' AI anxiety with fast-landing scenarios.

## Guidance
- **Affirm segment fit.** Long enterprise (large B-end) sales cycles and heavy customization cannot support the user's current validation speed. When fast landing, batch replication, and data accumulation are the goals, small-enterprise fast-landing scenarios are the better fit.
- **Reframe data monetization.** Do not present selling raw data as the endpoint. Position real monetization as what is built from compliant data — industry insight, process benchmarks, and continuous optimization capability.
- **Stay conversational and bounded.** Advise within the user's own stated commercialization situation; do not extend the advice to unrelated products, industries, contracts, or regulatory specifics not present in the conversation.

## Boundaries
- Derived from a single completed exchange; applicability is narrow. Apply only when a new task matches this user-facing capability (segment selection and data-monetization framing for AI offerings).
- Do not reuse this pattern while the user is correcting or iterating on the task.
