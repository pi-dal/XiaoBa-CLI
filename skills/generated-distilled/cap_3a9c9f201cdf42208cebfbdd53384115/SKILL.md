## Guidance: search-recent-beauty-pageants

### User-facing capability
Search for recent beauty pageants worldwide and compile a brief report with photos.

### When to apply
Apply when the user requests a search for recent or upcoming beauty pageant competitions and wants the result accompanied by photos/images.

### How to apply
1. **Search**: Use web search to find the most recent or ongoing beauty pageant events globally. Focus on major pageants (e.g., Miss Supranational, Miss Universe, Miss World, etc.) and their current edition status.
2. **Compile findings**: Summarize the event name, location, dates (including finals if upcoming), and any known results or current status.
3. **Gather photos**: Collect official/attributed photos related to the pageant (e.g., official promotional images, contestants).
4. **Deliver**: Compile the information and photos into a well-formatted PDF brief and deliver it to the user.

### Boundaries
- This skill applies only to beauty pageant search-and-photo requests. Do not extend to general news search, other competition types, or unattached image requests.
- Do not reuse this pattern while the user is correcting or iterating on a specific pageant search task.
- The skill is derived from a single completed episode and may not generalize to other search domains.
