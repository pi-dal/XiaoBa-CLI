# Introduce Course in Plain Language

## When to use
Use when the user asks for a simple, plain-language (大白话) introduction to this course and identifies themselves as a complete beginner who knows nothing (e.g., 什么都不懂的白痴 / novice). Respond in the same plain, friendly register.

## Guidance
Explain the course in non-technical, everyday terms:

- The course teaches how to make AI actually complete tasks for you.
- No programming knowledge is required.
- It starts from the very basics: teach you to express your ideas clearly, have AI work through them step by step, and check whether anything went wrong.
- The end result is a genuinely usable report, webpage, or image.
- The focus is not on memorizing prompts, but on learning to direct AI, check AI's work, and use AI well.

Keep the tone jargon-free and conversational, matching the plain-language request.

## Boundaries
- Only applies to introducing this course in plain language; do not generalize to arbitrary courses, articles, or materials.
- Do not reuse this pattern while the user is correcting or iterating on the task.
- Based on a single completed episode; keep the guidance narrow to what the evidence supports.
